Meet your counsellor

Hi, I’m Scott.

I believe good therapy begins with feeling genuinely seen. My role isn’t to hand you a script for your life, it’s to listen carefully, ask useful questions, and help you notice the strengths and possibilities that can be difficult to see from inside a hard season.

My approach is relational, trauma-informed, and grounded in evidence-based practice. Sessions are conversational and collaborative. We may explore how past experiences show up today, practise new ways of responding, or simply create enough space for you to hear yourself more clearly.

I work with adults and young adults from across BC, both in person and virtually.

Common questions

Before we begin

How do I know if counselling is right for me?

You don’t need to be in crisis or know exactly what you want to change. A consultation gives us a low-pressure way to talk about what is happening and whether my approach feels like a good fit.

What happens in the first session?

We’ll talk about what brought you in, what you hope might be different, and any context that feels important. You can ask questions, set the pace, and decide what you are comfortable sharing.

Do you offer virtual appointments?

Yes. Secure video sessions are available to clients located anywhere in BC at the time of the appointment.

Is counselling covered by insurance?

Many extended-health plans cover counselling with a Canadian Certified Counsellor, but plans vary. Check with your provider before booking if reimbursement is important to you.

What if I need urgent help?

This practice does not provide crisis services. If you are in immediate danger, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency department. In Canada, call or text 988 for 24/7 suicide crisis support.
